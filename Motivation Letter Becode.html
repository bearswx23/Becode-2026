01 — My story

From dashboards I read
to systems I'll build

My career started at Procter & Gamble, where I worked hands-on with sell-in/sell-out data, KPI monitoring, and market analysis to support commercial strategy. It evolved through AISIN Europe (Toyota Group automotive OEM), Hilti, and METHOD — always in commercial roles, always with structured data at the centre.

Whether it was demand planning interfaces, pricing compliance tracking, contract cost modelling, or quarterly P&L, every decision I recommended was built on analysis. But I was always one layer above the source — consuming reports, not engineering them. Dependent on tools others had built.

In 2020, I stepped back deliberately. I managed an independent multi-asset investment portfolio — real capital, live market data, structured risk frameworks. I developed disciplined approaches to performance tracking and systematic market analysis under real conditions, with real consequences.

That experience made one thing clear: I wasn't the person who couldn't understand data. I was the person who had never been handed the right tools. BeCode is where that changes.

What BeCode offers that fits me

Learner-driven methodology. I don't perform well in passive classrooms. I need to own the problem. BeCode's project-based structure puts the learner at the centre — that's where I work best.
Real company projects from month 3. Four months on actual business cases — no safety net. I've spent 8 years in that environment. I'm not intimidated. I'm ready.
Ends with a company internship. I want to apply immediately, not theorise. The built-in 1–3 month internship means I leave with professional proof, not just a certificate.
Free and inclusive. Democratising data literacy is the most important thing Belgium can do for its digital future. I'm proud to be part of that model.

What I bring to the cohort

Commercial translation layer. I can explain why an analysis matters to a CEO. Most data science cohorts lack someone who speaks both languages — technical and business. I bridge that gap from day one.
International market expertise. Direct experience in Gulf and North African B2B markets. In a data role, that opens client conversations most Belgian profiles simply cannot have.
Professional discipline. 8+ years of structured commercial accountability. I commit seriously, show up consistently, and push the people around me to do the same.
Clarity of purpose. I'm not here to try something new. I've made a deliberate, considered decision. BeCode is the structure I need — not the permission slip.
